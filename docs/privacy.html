Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

The short version: Jabe has no account system and collects no personal data about you. Your journal entries never leave your device. The messages you send in chat are passed to the Groq AI service to generate a reply — that, plus mood labels if you request a Weekly Insight, is everything that leaves your phone. We do not track you, sell your data, or require an account.

Overview

Jabe Wellness AI ("Jabe", "the app", "we") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information the app handles, how it is used, and your rights as a user.

Information We Do Not Collect

Jabe never collects, receives, or stores:

  • Your name, email address, or any account information
  • Your location
  • Your device identifiers
  • Advertising or tracking identifiers
  • Any biometric data

There is no Jabe server. The developer of this app receives none of your content — not your conversations, not your journal, not your moods.

What Stays On Your Device

The following is stored locally on your iPhone using Apple's on-device storage, and is never uploaded, backed up to a Jabe server, or shared with anyone:

  • Your saved chat session history
  • Your journal entries, including anything written in the Reframe Your Thoughts exercise
  • Your mood tracking data and check-in streak
  • App preferences and settings

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes all of this data.

What Leaves Your Device

Two things, both only in direct response to something you do:

  • Chat messages you send. The text goes to the Groq API to generate a reply, as described below. Your saved chat history stays on the device; only the conversation you are actively having is sent.
  • Mood labels and their dates, if you tap to generate a Weekly Insight. Never the text behind them.

AI Conversations (Groq API)

Jabe uses the Groq API to power its AI responses. When you send a message, the text of your conversation is transmitted to Groq's servers solely to generate a response. This transmission:

  • Carries no account, name, email, or device identifier — Jabe has no account system, so nothing links a message to you personally. The message text itself is whatever you choose to write, so please avoid including details you would not want a third-party AI provider to process.
  • Is not stored or used by Jabe beyond generating your response
  • Is subject to Groq's own privacy policy at groq.com/privacy

Jabe does not use your conversations for training, analytics, or any other purpose.

Weekly Insight

The optional Weekly Insight in the Insights tab is generated by the same Groq API. When you tap to generate one, Jabe sends only your recorded mood labels (for example "Anxious") and the dates they were recorded. The text of your journal entries and the contents of your saved conversations are not included and never leave your device.

Microphone & Speech Recognition

If you use the voice input feature, Jabe requests access to your microphone and uses Apple's speech recognition to transcribe your speech to text. Jabe explicitly requests on-device transcription, so on any device that supports it your voice audio is processed locally and never sent anywhere. On a device or language where Apple's on-device recognition is unavailable, iOS may transcribe using Apple's servers instead. In either case your voice audio is:

  • Never recorded, stored, or transmitted by Jabe
  • Handled entirely by Apple's speech recognition system, subject to Apple's privacy policy at apple.com/privacy

Notifications

If you enable daily reminders, Jabe schedules a local notification entirely on your device. No notification data is sent to any external server.

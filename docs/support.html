Support

Need help? Email jabelarde1994@gmail.com and describe what happened, which iPhone you're using, and your iOS version. Jabe is built and supported by one person, so replies usually take a day or two — every message is read.

Purchases

I bought Premium but the features are locked

Open Settings inside the app, tap the premium row to open the purchase screen, and tap Restore Purchase. Jabe Premium is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple Account, so it restores on any iPhone signed in to the same account.

I reinstalled the app and lost Premium

Use Restore Purchase as above. Your purchase is never lost — it lives with your Apple Account, not with the app.

What does Jabe Premium include?

  • Chat history — save and revisit every conversation
  • Journal and export
  • Mood insights and trends over time
  • Guided exercises — Box Breathing, Grounding, Reframe Your Thoughts
  • Voice input
  • AI weekly insights

Is it a subscription?

No. Jabe Premium is a single one-time purchase. There is no subscription and no recurring charge, ever.

I want a refund

Refunds are handled by Apple, not by Jabe. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com, sign in, and select the purchase. If Apple approves it, Jabe reflects the change automatically.

Your Data

Where is my information stored?

On your iPhone. Your journal entries, chat history, moods, and streak all stay on the device. There is no Jabe account and no Jabe server. Full detail is in the Privacy Policy.

How do I delete everything?

Delete the app from your iPhone. That permanently removes every journal entry, conversation, and mood record. Nothing is retained anywhere else.

Troubleshooting

Voice input isn't working

Voice input needs two permissions. Open iOS Settings → Jabe and confirm both Microphone and Speech Recognition are enabled. If you declined either when first asked, this is where you turn it back on.

Jabe isn't replying, or replies are slow

Replies need an internet connection. If you've sent many messages very quickly you may briefly hit a rate limit — wait about a minute and try again. If it persists, close and reopen the app.

The app is showing an error about the AI service

This usually means a temporary problem reaching the AI provider. Try again in a few minutes. If it continues for more than a day, please email so it can be looked into.

Is Jabe a therapist?

No. Jabe is not a licensed therapist, counsellor, or medical professional, and it does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. It is a supportive space to think out loud — not a substitute for professional care.

If you are in crisis, please reach a person, not an app.
United States — call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline), or text HOME to 741741 (Crisis Text Line).
Elsewhere — contact your local emergency services or a nearby crisis line. You deserve real support.
